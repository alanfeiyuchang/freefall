## description

```
Stand in your own room and reach for the bar. The moment you grab it, a transport plane grows out from your hand: the cargo hold, the red night lights, the open ramp, and beyond it the sky at 18,000 feet. Wait for the green light, let go, and you're falling.

Freefall is a first-person skydive for Apple Vision Pro, played with your own hands.

FALL
Accelerate to terminal velocity and drop through a raining cloud deck: mist rushing up past you, raindrops streaking by, your gloves and sleeves getting wet. Come out of the cloud base into the rain, and then into clear sky.

PULL
The altimeter is on your wrist. When it alarms at 3,500 feet, reach across with your other hand, grab the red handle on your forearm and pull. The pilot chute drags the canopy out, the lines snap tight and the cells inflate one by one above you. Pull too late and there won't be enough sky left.

FLY
Under canopy you steer by turning your head and body, and the canopy follows you. Grab the toggles to brake, or push them forward to dive. Tap the watch to bring up a helmet HUD with your altitude, heading and the landing spot. Land on the red-and-white circle to win; hit the yellow bullseye for a perfect landing.

SEVEN WORLDS
• Atoll – a white sandbar in a turquoise lagoon. Land short and you're swimming.
• Grassland – patchwork fields, a winding river and woods between you and the target.
• Alpine – a U-shaped valley whose walls rise above you as your canopy opens.
• Snow Peaks – thin air, so you fall faster and need to pull earlier.
• Island – open over the sea, fly in over the reef, and stay out of the rainforest.
• Volcano – an erupting cone, lava flows and an ash plume to steer clear of.
• Sky City – an abandoned stone city on floating islands above a sea of clouds.

Every jump has its own wind, so no two approaches are the same.

EARN YOUR GEAR
Every world has its own canopy, and its own gloves and sleeves, patterned after that world. Land on a world's target to unlock its canopy; hit the bullseye to unlock its gloves and sleeves.

PRACTICE AND REVIEW
Once you've landed safely in a world, start a jump at 5,000 feet to practice the canopy flight. After every jump, the route map shows your whole flight from above and whether you landed long or short, left or right.

COMFORTABLE BY DESIGN
• Best played seated, with headphones.
• By default you steer by turning your head and body, so the world never spins around you. Toggle steering is available as an option, with a motion-comfort warning.
• Automatic flare near the ground, or switch to manual and flare yourself.
• A jump takes about four minutes. Hold the watch for two seconds to quit at any time.

MADE IN REAL TIME
Apart from the gloves, everything you see and hear is generated as you play: the plane, the canopy, the clouds, the sea and the mountains. No accounts, no ads, no data collected, and it works offline. Available in ten languages.

Requires hand tracking.

Sound effects "wind 2", "parachute 2" and "nylon flapping 1" by Huggy13ear (freesound.org), licensed under CC BY 4.0.
```
